Source: GNT Daily Confirmation / broker record.

Date: September 8, 2026.

Evidence treatment: Broker-record values are reproduced below. The approximately $7,600.29 customer economic-equity figure is a separate reconstruction, not a field stated by the broker.

1. What the record says

The September 8 Daily Confirmation records the following account values:

Record fieldValue
Balance$100,331.83
Credit Facility$659,490.92
Floating P/L−$92,731.54
Displayed Equity$667,091.21
Margin Requirements$176,216.42
Available / Free Margin$490,874.79

Because the displayed equity includes the broker-provided credit facility, it is important not to treat the full $667,091.21 as customer money.

2. Reconstructed customer economic equity

Removing the $659,490.92 broker credit from the displayed equity leaves approximately $7,600.29 of reconstructed customer economic equity.

$667,091.21 displayed equity − $659,490.92 broker credit = $7,600.29

The same result is obtained from Balance plus Floating P/L:
$100,331.83 − $92,731.54 = $7,600.29.

Broker credit is not customer principal. I therefore do not count the $659,490.92 credit facility as invested capital or as an additional customer loss.

3. What this exhibit supports

This exhibit supports the September 8 broker-record values and the separate analytical reconstruction of approximately $7,600.29 of customer economic equity immediately before the later terminal account event.

It also shows why displayed account equity must be separated from broker credit when evaluating the customer's economic position.

4. What this exhibit does not prove

The $7,600.29 analytical figure should not be described as immediately withdrawable cash while positions remained open.

The September 8 Daily Confirmation also does not, by itself, explain the subsequent liquidation, establish why the account reached zero, or provide the complete accounting bridge from this snapshot to the terminal zero state.

5. Source and publication note

Public source copies should redact account numbers, email addresses, signatures, security information and unrelated personal information without removing context necessary to understand the record.
