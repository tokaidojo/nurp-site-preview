A NOTE ON THE STRATEGY NAMES

A note on the strategy names

The records support actual use of The FED, Yellen, Gold Digger/Gold, Odyssey, Argos, All Weather, Talos 5X and Talos 20X. Pelosi was configured but is not presently proven to have been funded and traded; Dalio appears in NURP's strategy environment, but a separate customer-specific Dalio account has not been established. The timeline therefore distinguishes verified use from incomplete historical leads.

The strategy names and broker names can make the history look more complicated than it is. This page keeps the sequence simple.

2023 — I became a NURP customer

I was looking for largely hands-off algorithmic trading. I began paying for NURP strategies and funding broker accounts through which the systems traded.

2024 — My capital expanded across broker accounts

I used NURP strategies at OX Securities and Monaxa. The Monaxa account is now reconstructed at $349,500.34 of lifetime economic depletion. The OX history is still being finalized because new records arrived September 22.

July 2025 — NURP presented a program with a stated 20% maximum-drawdown cap

The program was called AFT. NURP's presentation described 5x broker equity credit and stated that maximum drawdown was capped at 20% of initial account size. The presentation is preserved in Exhibit A.

September 2025 — Existing capital was moved into Talos

GNT records show $117,777.41 of existing capital moved into Talos 5X and $47,095.32 into Talos 20X. These were internal transfers, not new money I invested. Both Talos accounts ultimately went to zero.

Early 2026 — I reported an orphan-trade problem

An “orphan trade” is a copied trade left open in the customer account after it is no longer connected to the master strategy that was supposed to manage or close it. After a change involving the master trading account and broker, I reported that this had happened and that the issue had cost me more than $50,000. Later broker-history work supports further investigation, but the exact causal mapping remains incomplete.

August 2026 — NURP disclosed serious GNT execution problems

NURP reported that GNT customer accounts kept only about 30% of the trading performance produced by the master account and that 89% of customer trades were executed at worse prices than the corresponding master trades. GNT was the broker NURP required for the Talos and Argos strategies at issue in my experience, not a broker I independently chose. That combination made NURP's disclosure particularly significant and raised the question of what vetting and ongoing monitoring NURP performed before and while requiring customers to use GNT.

September 8, 2026 — The final verified Talos 5X snapshot

After excluding broker credit, the account reconstructs to approximately $7,600.29 of customer economic equity. A NURP Equity Monitor notice from the same date displayed a 70% equity-protect field and said no action had been taken on open trades or copiers.

September 2026 — Talos 5X reached zero and I exited

GNT later attributed the terminal event to a 90% margin stop-out followed by sequential liquidation and removal of broker credit. I requested the underlying transaction/server records. The two surviving Argos accounts ultimately totaled $112,668.13; both Talos accounts were at zero.

September 22, 2026 — New OX evidence changed my calculation

OX supplied new statements and transfer-routing information. The records materially changed the earlier OX reconstruction, so I retired the old public OX and overall figures rather than continue presenting them as current.
