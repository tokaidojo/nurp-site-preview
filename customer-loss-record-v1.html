METHODOLOGY

What qualifies for this record

1 · First-person public account

The customer describes their own NURP-related experience rather than repeating what happened to someone else.

2 · Material trading outcome

The account contains a concrete loss, severe drawdown, account depletion, reversal after gains, or another outcome relevant to evaluating live customer experience.

3 · Source preserved strongly enough

The underlying review or public post must be identifiable and sufficiently preserved to support the summary. Weak or unverifiable candidates remain out.

Evidence status: Unless otherwise stated, these are third-party customer accounts and have not been independently audited against brokerage statements. Where NURP posted a substantive response that can be confidently matched to the review, that response is summarized alongside it.

WHAT THIS RECORD DOES — AND DOES NOT — SHOW

Context, not a customer-wide statistic

These reports do not establish that every NURP customer experienced the same thing, that the same cause produced every loss, or what percentage of NURP's entire customer population was profitable or unprofitable. They establish the narrower point that substantial losses, severe drawdowns, reversals after profitable periods and accounts reportedly reaching zero appear in multiple public first-person customer accounts.

My own financial reconstruction does not depend on any of these reviews. It is based on brokerage statements, transaction histories, deposits and withdrawals, invoices and receipts, account records and contemporaneous communications.

EXCLUSIONS & HELD MATERIAL

What I deliberately did not count

Unverified identity / disputed customer status

Cases where customer status cannot be supported strongly enough remain out of the named record.

Hearsay or non-user commentary

Posts repeating another person's experience, generic accusations and commentary from people who say they did not use NURP are not counted as customer-loss cases.

Insufficiently preserved sources

Potentially significant cases—including a reported six-figure Reddit loss—remain on hold until the original source can be recovered and preserved strongly enough for publication.
