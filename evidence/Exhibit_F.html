September 8, 2026 — NURP Equity Monitor Notice

Status: Primary NURP record. The notice reports a 70% Equity Protect setting and states that no action was taken on copiers or open trades. The record does not define the calculation or action associated with that setting.

← Back to the Evidence Room

Source: NURP Equity Monitor notice.

Date: September 8, 2026.

Evidence treatment: The wording below is presented for what the notice itself states. Questions about how the 70% setting was calculated or intended to operate remain open.

1. What the notice says

The September 8 NURP notice reports:

“Equity monitor completed”

“Equity protect percentage: 70%”

“No action has been taken on any copiers copying to this account or open trades.”

The notice therefore documents both the displayed 70% Equity Protect setting and the reported result of the monitor at that time: no action on the copiers or open trades.

2. Why this record matters

The notice was generated at the end of the Talos 5X sequence, on the same date as the September 8 GNT Daily Confirmation used in Exhibit E.

That timing makes the notice relevant to understanding what risk-control or monitoring system was associated with the account immediately before the later terminal zero state.

3. What this exhibit supports

This exhibit supports saying that NURP's September 8 notice displayed an “Equity protect percentage: 70%” and reported that no action had been taken on copiers or open trades.

It also supports asking what that 70% setting meant, what reference value or denominator it used, what condition caused the monitor to complete, and what action—if any—it was configured to take.

4. What this exhibit does not prove

The notice does not define the starting balance or other reference used to calculate the 70% figure. It does not state what threshold or condition caused the monitor to complete, nor does it explain what action the monitor was configured to take.

It therefore should not be described as proof of a 70% drawdown guarantee or as proof that liquidation should have occurred at a particular account value.

The present record also does not establish whether this Equity Protect setting implemented, supplemented, or operated independently of the separate drawdown provision documented in the earlier AFT materials.

5. The open question

The documentary question is straightforward:

What did the 70% Equity Protect setting mean on Talos 5X, and why did the September 8, 2026 equity-monitor completion notice state that no action had been taken on open trades or copiers?

Answering that question requires the underlying configuration, calculation logic, trigger conditions, action rules, and contemporaneous system records. This exhibit does not supply those missing records.

6. Source and publication note

The original NURP notice is preserved separately. Any public source copy should redact account identifiers, email addresses, security information and unrelated personal information while preserving the wording and context necessary to understand the notice.
