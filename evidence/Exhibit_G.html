Public Evidence Room • Exhibit G

OX Records — January 2 Closing Cycle and the Still-Open Reconciliation

Status: Primary OX records; final OX lifetime result remains open. The available broker history supports a large January 2, 2025 closing loss. Additional OX records are still being reconciled, so this exhibit does not publish a final OX lifetime-loss figure.

← Back to the Evidence Room

Source: OX broker account histories, transaction records and later OX-supplied statements.

Evidence treatment: Confirm the transaction-level event that the records support; keep the broader OX lifetime result open until the remaining historical activity and routing are fully reconciled.

1. What the January 2 broker history shows

The OX history records a group of EUR/USD positions that had been opened during the period beginning September 30, 2024 and were closed on January 2, 2025.

Reconstructed net result of the documented January 2 closing cycle: approximately –$468,896.99.

That reconstruction includes the recorded trading result together with swap and commission entries associated with the documented positions.

2. Why this event matters

This is not an estimate based on a percentage or a recollection of an account balance. It is a reconstruction from the broker's transaction history for the identified closing cycle.

It therefore establishes a major documented loss event within the OX record. It does not, by itself, establish the final economic result of every OX account over the entire relationship.

3. What later OX records changed

Additional records supplied by OX resolved several previously open transfer questions and materially changed the broader OX reconstruction. That is why earlier working OX totals were retired rather than carried forward as though they were final.

The remaining work includes reconciling the complete late-2024 history and the archived account records needed to determine the OX ecosystem result without double-counting transfers or mistaking internal movements for new customer capital.

4. What this exhibit supports

The records support the approximately –$468,896.99 reconstructed net result for the documented January 2 closing cycle.

They also support treating the broader OX result separately from that single event and continuing the reconciliation before publishing a final lifetime OX loss.

5. What this exhibit does not prove

The January 2 loss does not by itself establish why the positions produced that result, who was responsible for the trading outcome, or whether a broker, strategy, execution issue, market event or some combination of factors caused the loss.

It also does not establish a final OX lifetime-loss figure. Deposits, withdrawals, internal transfers, other profitable and losing periods, and the remaining historical records must be reconciled before making that broader accounting statement.

6. Why no final OX total appears here

The purpose of this Evidence Room is to show what the records support without making the numbers larger—or more certain—than the evidence permits.

The OX reconciliation remains open. A final OX lifetime-loss number will not be presented here until the remaining records are reconciled, or it will be clearly labeled as an estimate if publication occurs before those records are available.

This treatment prevents internal transfers from being counted as fresh customer deposits and prevents an incomplete historical period from being presented as a completed lifetime result.

7. Source material

The underlying OX records are preserved separately. Public source copies should redact account identifiers and unrelated personal information while retaining the transaction information needed to understand the reconstruction.
