Orphan Trades — Recovered Working Record

Source status: Recovered public-redacted workbook available.

What the source record supports

The recovered March 3, 2026 workbook lists 61 positions: headings describe 32 for Argos #1 and 29 for Argos #2; at row level, 48 are explicitly labeled “Orphaned” and 13 “Open.” The 48 explicitly labeled positions total approximately –$16,575.12 at the values shown.

Publication limitation

The workbook materially corroborates that an orphan-trade problem was being tracked at the account level, but it is not a complete final orphan-trade damages calculation and should not be added separately to losses already embedded in the GNT economic result.
