AFT Webinar — 5x Equity Credit and 20% Drawdown Statement

Status: Primary presentation evidence. This exhibit documents what NURP presented about the AFT program on July 23, 2025. Whether the same drawdown provision later governed Talos remains an open question.

← Back to the Evidence Room

Source: NURP AFT webinar / preserved source frame.

Date: July 23, 2025.

Status: Primary presentation evidence — contractual application to Talos remains open.

1. What NURP presented

The preserved presentation describes AFT as using broker-provided equity credit to allow trading with five times more capital. The same presentation states:

“Maximum drawdown is capped at 20% of initial account size (profits can be accumulated to provide more room).”

This is evidence of how the AFT risk structure was presented by NURP at the time.

2. What this exhibit supports

The record supports the public statement that NURP presented AFT with a 5x broker equity-credit structure and a stated 20% maximum-drawdown cap.

That presentation matters because capital was later moved into Talos strategies, making the relationship between the earlier AFT risk presentation and the later Talos risk controls an important question in the record.

3. What this exhibit does not prove

The July 23 presentation does not by itself establish that the later Talos 5X or Talos 20X accounts were contractually governed by the same 20% drawdown provision.

The governing Talos terms therefore remain an open evidence item. This exhibit documents the earlier presentation; it does not convert that presentation into a conclusion about the contractual terms governing a later strategy.

4. Why the distinction matters

The evidence can answer two different questions, and they should not be collapsed into one:

What was presented? NURP presented AFT as using 5x broker equity credit with maximum drawdown capped at 20% of initial account size.

What later governed Talos? The currently available presentation evidence does not resolve that question by itself.

Keeping those questions separate allows the record to show the presentation clearly without overstating what it proves.

Public conclusion

The preserved July 23, 2025 NURP AFT presentation documents a 5x broker-equity-credit structure and states that maximum drawdown was capped at 20% of initial account size. The record supports publishing that presentation as evidence of how AFT risk protection was described. It does not, standing alone, establish that the later Talos accounts were governed by the identical provision.
