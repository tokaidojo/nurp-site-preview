PUBLIC EVIDENCE ROOM • EXHIBIT B

September 24, 2025 — Existing AFT Capital Moved into Talos

Status: Primary transfer evidence. This exhibit documents existing customer capital being moved internally from All Weather accounts into Talos 5X and Talos 20X. These transfers were movements of existing money, not new outside deposits.

← Back to the Evidence Room

Source: Original GNT internal-transfer email thread.

Date: September 24, 2025.

Status: Primary transfer evidence — capital movement established; Talos governing risk terms not established by this record.

1. What the record says

I asked GNT to move funds from three existing or closed All Weather accounts into two new accounts within the NURP AFT program. GNT first said it would complete the request and then confirmed that the internal transfers had been completed.

Talos 5X total transferred: $117,777.41.

Combined existing customer capital moved into Talos: $164,872.73.

2. Source-faithful excerpts

“I have two new accounts within the Nurp AFT program and would like to transfer funds from three existing/closed accounts into the new accounts.”
“Good morning, the internal transfers have been completed.”

3. What this exhibit supports

This exhibit directly supports the capital bridge from the earlier All Weather accounts into Talos 5X and Talos 20X.

It also supports treating the $164,872.73 as internal/recycled customer capital rather than as new outside deposits. Counting these transfers again as fresh investment would double-count money that was already inside the account ecosystem.

4. What this exhibit does not prove

The transfer email does not establish what contractual or technical risk-control terms governed Talos after the transfer.

It also does not prove that the earlier AFT drawdown provisions carried forward unchanged. That remains a separate evidence question.

Public conclusion

The September 24, 2025 GNT transfer record documents $164,872.73 of existing customer capital moving from All Weather accounts into Talos 5X and Talos 20X. For the loss reconstruction, these amounts are therefore treated as recycled/internal capital, not as additional external investment.

Why this matters to the accounting

This transfer trail is one of the controls used to avoid inflating the loss reconstruction. Money that moved from one strategy account to another is counted as the same customer capital, not as a second investment.
