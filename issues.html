The Issues That Matter, in Plain English

These are the questions I would want answered before deciding whether to entrust money to an automated trading program.

NURP later said its GNT customers were getting much worse results than the master trades

NURP reported that GNT customer accounts kept only about 30% of the trading performance produced by the master account, roughly 70% of expected returns was lost to execution costs, and 89% of customer trades were executed at worse prices than the corresponding master trades. I present those as NURP's findings, not as a formula for my personal damages. GNT was the broker NURP required for the Talos and Argos strategies at issue in my experience, rather than a broker I independently selected. That raises an important question: what responsibility did NURP have to vet and monitor the execution quality of a broker its customers were required to use?

I reported more than $50,000 in losses from an orphan-trade problem

An “orphan trade” is a copied trade that remains open in the customer account after it is no longer connected to the master strategy that was supposed to manage or close it. I reported this problem after a master-account/broker transition. Later broker-history work supports further investigation, but the missing master/copier mapping prevents an exact causal loss figure.
