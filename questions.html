Orphan-trade mapping

An orphan trade is a copied trade left open after it is no longer connected to the master strategy that was supposed to manage or close it. The remaining question requires the records showing how the master strategy and customer accounts were linked needed to determine exactly which customer-account trades became orphaned.

Why keep unresolved questions public?

Because a prospective customer may care not only about the final dollar result, but also about how brokers were selected, how risk controls were supposed to work, how copied trades were managed during account transitions, and what records exist when an account fails. If additional records answer these questions, this page should change with them.

Evidence and correction policy. This site distinguishes primary records, reconstructed amounts, party explanations and unresolved questions. Documentary corrections that materially change a factual statement or calculation will be dated and incorporated.
