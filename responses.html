A SIMPLE STANDARD FOR ANY RESPONSE

Whatever the explanation, start with the documented result

I was a NURP customer for approximately three years. I paid substantial fees for access to NURP algorithms and placed substantial capital into accounts where those algorithms traded. I followed the broker arrangements associated with the strategies, including using GNT for the Talos and Argos strategies at issue in my experience. The financial result was an enormous loss of my capital.

That fact alone does not establish the cause of every dollar lost. Trading itself involves risk, and this site does not treat every losing trade as proof that NURP caused the loss. Instead, I have tried to separate documented outcomes from questions of causation.

If responsibility is attributed to me, I believe the explanation should identify the evidence.

What action did I take? When did I take it? Which account, strategy or trades did it affect? What contemporaneous record documents the action? What evidence connects that action to the claimed loss? And how much of the loss does the evidence actually explain?

The same principle applies to broker explanations. For the Talos and Argos strategies at issue in my experience, GNT was the broker NURP required customers to use. If execution quality at that broker materially affected customer results—as NURP later reported—then I believe a meaningful response should address the selection, vetting and monitoring of that required broker, as well as when the execution problem became known and what action followed.

I will evaluate a substantive response against the underlying records. If records supplied by NURP or a broker show that something on this site is materially wrong, I will correct it.

NURP and the brokers should have a meaningful opportunity to respond

NURP, GNT, OX, Monaxa or another source may provide a substantive response to a factual statement, calculation or evidence interpretation on this site. A useful response should identify the statement at issue and provide the supporting record or explanation. Material responses may be reproduced or fairly summarized with the source and date identified.

If a response includes records that materially change my reconstruction, I will change the public record. The September 22 OX update is an example of that principle in practice.

Evidence and correction policy. This site distinguishes primary records, reconstructed amounts, party explanations and unresolved questions. Documentary corrections that materially change a factual statement or calculation will be dated and incorporated.
