Before You Give NURP Your Money, I Believe You Should See What Happened to Mine.

I used NURP's automated trading algorithms for nearly three years across multiple brokers and accounts.

Some algorithms made money for a time. Some lost money. Some made substantial profits before suffering much larger losses. Surviving capital was sometimes moved from one NURP strategy into another.

This website documents what happened using my brokerage records, transaction histories, NURP communications and other contemporaneous evidence.

You don't have to take my word for it. The underlying records are available throughout this site.

WHY I STAYED

If Things Were Going Badly, Why Did I Stay?

Because they weren't always going badly.

Some of these algorithms made substantial money before they lost money. FED generated approximately $221,448 in cumulative profits through 2024 before the later loss cycle overwhelmed those gains. My earlier Argos account was profitable. My later GNT Argos accounts generated substantial profits during 2025. Gold made money. Even Talos 5X and Talos 20X initially made money.

Those profitable periods reinforced my confidence and help explain why I continued.

The complete story isn't that nothing ever worked. It is what happened to the money over the entire relationship.

WHAT THIS SITE DOES NOT CLAIM

Result and cause are not the same thing.

Trading involves risk. A financial loss by itself does not prove wrongdoing.

I do not assume that every dollar I lost was caused by NURP, a broker or an execution problem. I do not count internal transfers as new investments. I do not call an account a trading wipeout merely because its balance later became zero after money was withdrawn or transferred. And where the evidence does not establish an answer, I say so.

My objective is simpler: document what happened, compare it with what was represented, show the underlying records, and let readers reach their own conclusions.
